The Government of India constituted a **High-Level Committee** on Demographic Change to study population shifts arising from illegal immigration and other abnormal factors, and to recommend measures to address them.

📌 KEY FACTS FOR RPSC

📰 WHY IN NEWS
The Union Government notified the **National Sports Governance (National Sports Board) Rules 2026** and the **National Sports Governance (National Sports Tribunal) Rules 2026** under the **National Sports Governance Act 2025**.

📌 KEY FACTS FOR RPSC
- The **National Sports Governance Act** was enacted in **2025** — India's first comprehensive legislation to regulate governance of national sports federations.
- The Act creates two distinct bodies: **National Sports Board (NSB)** — the apex regulatory and oversight body for sports federations — and **National Sports Tribunal (NST)** — a quasi-judicial body to adjudicate sports-related disputes.
- **NSB** is under the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports; it oversees compliance of national sports federations with governance norms including elections, age limits, and transparency.
- The Act applies to National Sports Federations (**NSFs**) that receive government grants or recognition, and is India's legislative response to the International Olympic Committee's demand for clean sports governance.

💡 MEMORY HOOK
**NSB** runs the game, **NST** settles the dispute — Board governs, Tribunal adjudicates, both born from **Act 2025**.

🏛 Indian Economy › Index of Industrial Production › IIP Base Year Revision 2022-23
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
📰 WHY IN NEWS
**MoSPI** announced the revision of the **IIP** base year from **2011-12** to **2022-23**, with the new series to be released on 1 June 2026 — the most significant IIP revision in over a decade.

📌 KEY FACTS FOR RPSC
- **IIP (Index of Industrial Production)** measures the quantum of industrial output across Mining, Manufacturing, and Electricity sectors; published monthly by **MoSPI** (formerly CSO).
- Base year history: **1993-94** → **2004-05** → **2011-12** → **2022-23** (effective June 2026) — each revision updates the basket of goods and weights to reflect current industrial structure.
- The 2022-23 revision will increase the number of items tracked, update sector weights, and align with post-COVID industrial realities; Manufacturing continues to carry the highest weight (approximately **77%**).
- IIP is a key high-frequency indicator used by **RBI** for monetary policy decisions and by the government to track industrial health between two GDP releases.

💡 MEMORY HOOK
IIP base years: **93-94, 04-05, 11-12, 22-23** — MoSPI publishes, RBI uses, Manufacturing dominates at **~77%**.

📰 WHY IN NEWS
The **Department of Telecommunications (DoT)** introduced major citizen-friendly reforms to the **PM-WANI** framework, simplifying public Wi-Fi access by removing barriers like mandatory app downloads and registrations.

📌 KEY FACTS FOR RPSC
- **PM-WANI** stands for Prime Minister Wi-Fi Access Network Interface — launched by **DoT** in **December 2020** to create a decentralised public Wi-Fi ecosystem across India.
- The framework operates through three entities: **PDO** (Public Data Office — the local Wi-Fi hotspot provider), **PDOA** (Public Data Office Aggregator — manages multiple PDOs), and App Provider (authenticates users); no licence fee is required to become a PDO.
- Key feature: PM-WANI was designed to allow small shopkeepers and local entrepreneurs to become Wi-Fi providers (PDOs) without any licence, enabling last-mile broadband access.
- After **2026** reforms, users can connect to PM-WANI hotspots without downloading a separate app or completing fresh registration each time — friction-free access aligned with Digital Public Infrastructure goals.

💡 MEMORY HOOK
**PM-WANI** = Wi-Fi for All Nationally by India — DoT, **December 2020** — **PDO** sells, **PDOA** aggregates, App authenticates.
